## Task completed

- Audited the whole chain (code, tests, documents) from the minimal runtime. Two gaps stood out: a whole program could not be written in C++, and the front end, the stripping script, and the driver were three disconnected steps.
- Merged `cpp-codegen-operators`, making C++ a program language. A program enters MLIR through ClangIR and is split per host function: the host keeps its symbol and C++ signature, operators become independent units, results are written to destination parameters, and shapes come from parameter types.
- Deleted the semi-automatic path; annotations now express only the operator and its placement preference.
- Unified the entry point. `skeleton++` chains clang++, `cir-opt`, and the driver; `mlir-skeleton-driver` splits units, rewrites operator calls into a resolve plus an indirect call, JITs the host, recompiles a unit on a cache miss, and falls back to the CPU.
- Strengthened acceptance. GPU cases assert that the operator actually ran on the device rather than comparing numbers only, and synchronization waits on the context the kernel runs in.
- Ran the three groups of skeleton tests on the remote GPU host; all pass. The timings are one-off debug-build records, used as a trend only.
